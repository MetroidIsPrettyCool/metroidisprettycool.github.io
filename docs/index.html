1. Introduction

1.1. Introduction Introduction Introduction

Hi! Welcome to my #webpresence.

There’s not much here, which is sort of deliberate. Just wanted to replace the nonsense that was here previously wit something that isn’t obviously unfinished. Maybe I’ll expand later, maybe I won’t. At the moment I have no current plans to do besides GitHub Pages-squat.

You can find me most places under the username “metroidisprettycool”, “metroidipc”, “mipc”, or some variation thereof. It’s unprofessional, clearly, but I made my first real “social media” account (Reddit, since deleted) under that name and I value consistency more than I value being professional.

1.2. What’s Your Deal?

Here’s a collation of all my stances on the usual trivial debates.

I tend to pendulum around between “computer nerd stuff”, “aerospace nerd stuff”, and watching lots of movies while I fantasize about bombing the Map-Territory distinction.

As a computer nerd, I like most of the usual computer nerd things. Programming, maintaining my franken-Arch Linux installation, arguing, video games, PC-building, arguing, reading Douglas Hofstadter books and stroking my chin very thoughtful-like,1 arguing…

As a space nerd, I have a big soft spot for the space shuttle. So, very, very, very flawed. But at that time, under those governments, knowing then only what we knew then? I think we’d have been hard-pressed to do better.

As a fan of the arts, you’ll find my movie opinions on Letterboxd.2 I like bold stuff. The unapologetic and hard-to-swallow. Big ideas. Meta-commentary. That sort of thing.
